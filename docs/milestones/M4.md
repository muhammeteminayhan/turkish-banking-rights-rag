# M4 — Generation + citation verification

Status: **done** (2026-10-29). The smoke set ran end to end with Ollama + Qwen3 8B: six
runs, one of them invalid (GPU memory, below). Your decisions after the runs are in place:
thinking on by default, and a missing fıkra in a citation is accepted. No paid API call
was made.

**Bottom line.** The pipeline works and no unverified citation reaches the user. With the
final settings, 17 of 20 answers were verified, all 3 out-of-scope questions were refused,
every verified answer cites an expected article, and by my reading 12 of the 17 are
correct, 4 partly correct and 1 wrong. With thinking off, 4 of 12 were correct. A verified
citation does not make the answer right: the checks prove that the quote is in the cited
article, not that the conclusion follows from it.

## How an answer is checked

Nothing the model writes is shown until it passes these checks; otherwise it gets one retry
with the errors, and a second failure is withheld ("doğrulanamadı"):

1. Every citation's `chunk_id` is one of the passages **shown** to the model (passages over
   the prompt budget are not shown and cannot be cited).
2. Its `doc_id`, `madde` and `fikra` match that chunk ("28/A" = "28A"; a fıkra range
   "2-5" accepts 2…5). A **missing** fıkra is accepted (your decision, 2026-10-29): the
   label shown comes from the chunk ("md. 4/3"), and the quote must be in that fıkra. A
   wrong fıkra, or one the chunk does not have, is still an error.
3. Its quote (case and whitespace normalised, at least 15 characters) occurs in the chunk's
   text or in one of its notes, never across the two. The user sees the **source span**,
   not the model's spelling.
4. The answer text is not empty, and **every number in it occurs in a cited chunk** (text,
   notes, label, article, fıkra) or in the question. Numbers are compared as values:
   "otuz gün" = "30 gün", "on beş" = 15, "ikiyüzelli" = 250, "8.300" = 8300, dates by
   day/month/year. This catches an invented "%5" or "md. 73" in an otherwise cited answer.
5. An out-of-scope answer has no citations; any other answer has at least one.

A model refusal is not retried; it is withheld like a failed answer.

What the mistakes look like:
- **Prohibition reversed** (b1-007 in the first run: "reddedebilir" where the source says
  "reddedilemez"). Rule 8 ("do not invert a prohibition") fixed it in every later run.
- **Old amount instead of the footnote's current one** (b1-033: 1–2 TL from the article
  instead of 7,97 TL from the 6/1/2026 table). Rule 7 worked only with thinking, and then
  the model took the wrong column (15,96 TL is the 8.300,01–399.000 TL band).
- **A rule applied to the wrong product** (b1-060: the konut kredisi 1 %/2 % compensation
  cap given for an ihtiyaç kredisi). Rule 9 did not fix it without thinking.
- **False premise confirmed** (b1-064 without thinking: the katılım bankası wording
  "gecikme faizi … gecikme cezası olarak uygulanır" read as permission for an extra fee).
  With thinking the premise is corrected.
- **Self-contradiction** (b1-024, b1-036 without thinking: "masrafları geri alamazsınız"
  next to the 7-day refund; "durum değişmez" next to "no fee for installments").

**Why the numbers dropped without thinking:** the greedy run scored the same as the
T 0.7 run, so the sampling change did not cause it. Most likely the prompt change did
(the first prompt, without rules 7–10, did better without thinking: 9 of 14 vs 4 of 12).
Not fully isolated: the retrieval models also moved from the GPU (fp16) to the CPU (fp32),
which can reorder close passages, and n = 20. One regression is clear: **the model writes
`fikra: null`** for chunks whose fıkra is known. That was the only error in 4 of 6 withheld answers without thinking and in all
3 with thinking. It happens for printed numbers too (6502 md. 4/3), not only inferred ones.

**Refusals:** the top reranker score of the 3 out-of-scope questions was 0.037–0.060 in
every run; the lowest answerable question scored 0.58. Without thinking, b1-051 (out of
scope, top score 0.037) was answered with citations and only failed on `fikra: null`;
with thinking it was refused. A score threshold would have caught it (to validate in M5;
3 questions are not evidence).

### The GPU bottleneck (and the invalid run)

The run on 2026-10-28 evening took 98 minutes and 12 of 20 questions hit the 300 s
timeout. `ollama ps` said the model was 100 % on the GPU the whole time. Measured the next
day with the same 3.2k-token prompt:

| Setup | Ollama output | Prompt reading (cold) | Rerank 30 passages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ollama alone | 54 tok/s | 2,053 tok/s | – |
| bge-m3 + reranker on the GPU, then Ollama loads (the smoke order) | **33 tok/s** | **216 tok/s** | 1.0 s |
| Ollama loaded first, then the two models | 54 tok/s | – | 1.0 s (0.2 s alone) |
| bge-m3 + reranker on the CPU | 55 tok/s | 2,211 tok/s | 11.2 s → **4.9 s** sorted |

The context window has the same limit. These are Ollama's own figures (`ollama ps`),
with the retrieval models on the CPU:

| `num_ctx` | Model size | On the GPU |
|---|---|---|
| 8,192 | 5,907 MiB | 5,907 MiB (all) |
| 9,216 | 6,448 MiB | 5,915 MiB |
| 10,240 | 6,598 MiB | 5,898 MiB |
| 12,288 | 6,889 MiB | 6,002 MiB (b1-001: 69 s instead of 43 s) |

Ollama (5.9 GB at 8k context) plus the two models (2.3 GB) do not fit in 8 GB. Windows
does not fail: it moves memory into shared system RAM, and the GPU slows down. Ollama
does not report it, and on a busier desktop the slowdown reached the timeouts. The first
run was affected too (b1-001: 68 s then, 36 s now).

Fixes:
- **`RETRIEVAL_DEVICE=cpu` by default** when answering. The GPU is the language model's.
  Embedding a question takes 0.07 s on the CPU. The reranker takes ~5 s for 30 candidates
  now that they are scored shortest first in batches of 4 (the scores do not change). The
  retrieval eval and search CLIs still use the GPU (no LLM there).
- **The smoke run stops after 2 model errors in a row**, keeps the partial report marked
  "Stopped early", exits 1 and points to `ollama ps` and Task Manager's shared GPU memory.
  It logs each question as it finishes, so a slow run shows in the first minute.
- Reports record the decoding options and the retrieval device.

Not done: `OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0` with flash attention would shrink Ollama by ~0.6 GB
and might let the reranker go back on the GPU (−5 s per question). It is a setting of the
Ollama server on your machine, and it is tight on an 8 GB card that also runs other apps.

## Anthropic provider (no call made)

Written from the `claude-api` skill: model `claude-opus-5-5` ($4 / $20 per million input /
output tokens); structured output through `output_config.format` (JSON schema) with
`effort: medium`. **Refusal fallback is on by default** (`fallbacks: "default"`, beta
`server-side-fallback-2026-07-01`): if Claude declines, the API re-runs the request on
Anthropic's recommended fallback model; the answering model is recorded and its cost is
reported as unknown when it is not Opus 5.5. Credentials: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`,
`ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN` (also from `.env`) or an `ant auth login` profile. The SDK is the
optional extra `pip install -e .[anthropic]`; it is not installed.

**Cost estimate, if you want Claude for the smoke run:** the measured prompts are ~4.8k
input tokens per question including retries (96k for the 20); answers with adaptive
thinking perhaps 1–2k output tokens, so about 20 × (4.8k × $4 + 1.5k × $20) / 1M ≈
**$1.00**, up to ~$1.50 with retries. Not run.

## Decisions

Decided on 2026-10-29 ("önerilerine göre devam et"):
1. **Thinking on by default.** The plan said thinking off for structured output. Measured,
   thinking is clearly better (10 vs 4 correct of the verified answers, 3/3 refusals) at
   ~6–9 s more per question. `OLLAMA_THINK=0` turns it off.
2. **A missing fıkra is accepted** (option (a)). The other options were dropping
   `doc_id`/`madde`/`fikra` from the schema, or keeping the check strict.
3. **Refusal threshold** on the top reranker score: not turned on. The out-of-scope tops
   were 0.037–0.060 against ≥ 0.58 for answerable questions. To be measured on the 25
   out-of-scope questions of M5 first.
