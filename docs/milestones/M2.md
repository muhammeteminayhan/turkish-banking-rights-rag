## What was built

| Piece | File |
|---|---|
| mevzuat.gov.tr HTML → blocks: paragraphs, data tables, amendment-history tables, `[n]` footnotes, `<sup>` endnotes, separators; layout tables flattened; punctuation repaired after span joins | `src/finhak/parsing/blocks.py` |
| Blocks → KISIM/BÖLÜM, articles (MADDE / Madde / GEÇİCİ / EK, `28/A`), titles, fıkra, bent, mülga, amendment notes; tail split into endnotes, footnotes, history and annexes | `src/finhak/parsing/structure.py` |
| TCMB talimat PDF → sections `n)`, bents `a.`, footnotes **with their tables**, Ek-1 items (pdfplumber, by font size) | `src/finhak/parsing/talimat.py` |
| Structures → chunks with stable ids, resolved notes, snapshot dates | `src/finhak/parsing/chunker.py` |
| PDF page lookup + independent article count; parse report; CLI | `pages.py`, `report.py`, `cli.py`, `scripts/parse_sources.py` |

Output: `data/processed/chunks.jsonl`. One JSON line per chunk with these fields:

- **Identity:** `id`, document identity.
- **Location:** unit, madde, madde title, fıkra, `fikra_inferred`, bents, part, kısım/bölüm.
- **Content:** `text`, `notes`, amendments, `mulga`, `pages`.
- **Provenance:** `source_url`, `rg_date` / `version_date`, `collected_on`.

The `version_date` and `collected_on` fields carry the decision that answers show the snapshot date.

## Code review

`ecc:python-reviewer` reviewed the package before the first commit. No CRITICAL findings, no security findings. Fixed, test first:

| Finding (HIGH) | Fix |
|---|---|
| Unnumbered laws over-split: every capitalised line started a fıkra (5411 md. 3 became 33 "fıkra") | A line after one ending in `,` `;` `:` continues the fıkra; 5411 fıkra 707 → 666 |
| "(Değişik birinci fıkra:…)" in a heading was copied to every fıkra | Notes naming a fıkra/bent/cümle/ibare go to the first fıkra only |
| "Madde 129 —." and "MADDE 38 – 39 –" shifted fıkra numbers / invented text | Dash may be followed by "."; two numbers on one heading become a range article `m38-39` |
| Any line starting with "Ek" or in capitals after the last article ended it | Annex headings must be a line of their own; capital titles may not end with "." |
| A repeal note on fıkra 1 was lost from a repealed article's chunk | Fıkra notes are kept on the article chunk |

Also fixed (MEDIUM): leading notes in titles; `mulga` now covers "Başlığı ile birlikte Mülga" and İptal; later parts of a split fıkra are located on their own pages; footnotes/endnotes nobody cites are listed in the report. Left open (documented): notes of a split fıkra are copied to all its parts; `<sup>`/`[n]` inside table cells are not resolved (one case: 5411 footnote 72).

## Issues found and how they were handled

1. **Superscripts are endnotes, not history rows.** `<sup>(n)</sup>` in regulations points to the "(n) Bu değişiklik … yürürlüğe girer." notes after the last article. It does not point to the amendment-history table, which I had assumed at first. Laws put footnote numbers in `<sup>13</sup>` / `<sup>[32]</sup>` / `[n]`.
2. **Layout tables.** Hakem Heyetleri and Erişilebilirlik sit entirely in single-column tables, and their first parse found 0 articles. Single-column tables are now read through.
3. **Source quirks, kept as published.**
   - `( 6)` for (6) in 6502 m.51.
   - `( Değişik:RG -…)`.
   - "(2) ila (5)" ranges.
   - Ek-1 of the talimat numbers the first item of group 3 as "2.1." (reported as a warning, not corrected).
4. **Source error, reported and not guessed.** Tüketici Kredisi Yönetmeliği has two endnotes both numbered "(1)", while the text cites (1) and (2). Its chunks say "[belirsiz …]" / "[kaynakta bulunamadı]" (9 ambiguous and 5 unresolved references in the report).
5. **Span joins.** The text is joined with spaces across Word spans, which detached punctuation ("sistemlerine ,"). A Turkish punctuation tidy step was added and measured: 0 such cases remain.
6. **Known limits.**
   - Seven chunks exceed 2,000 characters: long fıkra without bents, and big annex tables that are one block. They are listed in the report and left for M3 to judge.
   - Three regulations have no PDF on mevzuat.gov.tr, so their chunks have no page.
